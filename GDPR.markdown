## GDPR Campaign

*Brief: Create an engaging campaign concept for an upcoming change to Government Data Protection Regulations (GDPR)*

The team came up with the concept of using sport and fitness as a metaphor for business adapting to the changing environment. Making their organisation and data 'fit' for the new regulatory environment and selling the benefits of being 'data fit' to the business. We wanted to make the campaign fun to stand out from the plain GDPR campaigns in the space already but still maintain a serious, credible edge as this was to be a business facing campaign.

My first attempt at "Mr Data Fit".

![alt text](/img/gdpr1.jpg "GDPR workings")

"Mr Data Fit" in some early example mockups.

After talking with the team about the direction this was going we decided that the mascot and general tone was too light and needed to get more serious. In this discussion I argued we should ditch 'Get data fit' as a strapline in favour of 'GDPR 2018' to simplify the campaign.

![alt text](/img/gdpr3.png "GDPR workings")

With this more serious tone in mind I began to develop an idea that merged the sport with the idea of data.
