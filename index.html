About Me

I am a designer and developer who loves to learn, design and make things.

I run a user experience blog over at northerndiv.com and have a healthy flow of freelance design and development work. I have also created projects such as sheftype.co.uk, trakrific.com and a front end speed monitoring tool.

I am extremely creative and driven. I love to create things in my spare time as an outlet for creative ideas and technical curiosity.

I am an excellent communicator, often giving presentations to my team on things I have learned.

I love to work as part of a team but am also extremely capable of working under my own initiative, meeting deadlines and coping under pressure.

Skills

Proficient with Photoshop, Illustrator and good knowledge of After Effects and Premiere Pro.

Confident and able to hand code HTML, CSS (SASS) and Javascript. I've recently been using Backbone.js a lot and have used Angular.js and React.js in the past. I also have a good understanding of PHP frameworks Codeigniter and Symfony.

Content management systems I have developed with include Joomla, Wordpress, Magento and Fuel CMS.

I have skills in planning, running and analysing usability studies and I am a keen advocate of UX design.

I have a particular obsession with speed and use tools such as Speedcurve to ensure I am creating the fastest, leanest sites possible. I use Gulp or Grunt to minify CSS and concatentate and minify JS as well as ensuring resources are gzipped on the server.

I am constantly trying to create, improve and learn
