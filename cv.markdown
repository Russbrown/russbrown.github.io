## CV

I am a ux designer and front end developer from Sheffield who loves to learn, design and make things. 

I love design, particularly UI design, and have been designing interfaces for over 4 years. I am a big advocate of a user centred design process. 

I have built and managed small to large CSS codebases and worked on very small and very large teams. I am a big fan of BEM, task runners and modular CSS. I love reading about the continued development of CSS. I also have excellent javascript skills, both vanilla and jquery and have dabbled with a few frameworks though I'm no expert.

I like to learn and explore things through side projects and run a blog over at [theprdct.com](http://www.theprdct.com) where I interview interesting designers and founders. 

In the past, though I've taken them down now I created a tool to monitor keywords on Twitter and in the news called [trakrific.com](https://www.behance.net/gallery/27586215/Trakrificcom) and a front end [speed monitoring tool.](https://www.behance.net/gallery/28356499/Performance-Monitor) amongst others. I also used to maintain a blog about Sheffield's found typography as well as a UX blog. I was once featured in [Sidebar!](http://sidebar.io/)


## Experience
*March 2013 - October 2014*

**UX Designer & Front End Developer at Stoneacre Motor Group.**

Responsibilities included design, UX and front end development. The key achievement here was delivering a new website which quadrupled finance applications, increased traffic dramatically as well as significantly improved the user experience. The site was the fastest main dealer website in the UK. I also delivered a prestige car site, a specialist MOT site and a custom CMS alongside many other web and creative projects.

*October 2014 - October 2015*

**UX Designer & Front End Developer at Plus.net**

Responsibilities included front end development and UX design. I delivered a flagship careers website, made numerous UX improvements to the main user journey and won the company’s Team of the Year award.

*October 2015 - Present day*

**Creative Designer & Front End Developer at Irwin Mitchell Solicitors**

Responsibilities include campaign concepts, ux design & front end development. 

I designed and developed numerous campaign projects such as a [Cyber Security Quiz](https://www.behance.net/gallery/51703189/Cyber-Security-Quiz), a [UK Powerhouse quiz](http://www.irwinmitchell.com/powerhousequiz/index.html) and [GDPR Campaign concept and web page](https://irwinmitchell.com/gdpr-2018).

I redesigned [the office pages](http://www.irwinmitchell.com/our-offices/united-kingdom/birmingham) to be faster and easier to use. I also redesigned [the main navigation,](https://irwinmitchell.com/) clearing out all the legacy CSS & JS and delivering a faster, leaner solution that was easier to use, faster to load and far easier to maintain. 

I also produced a brand guidelines site internally to provide assets to the design team more easily and a pattern library and CSS standards to begin to modernise and manage the legacy codebase.

I also undertook pure UX work such as user interviews, journey mapping and usability studies to drive change in the organisation.
