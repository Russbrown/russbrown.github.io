[PlanYourDay.cc](https://planyourday.cc/)

Inspired by [this Notebook](https://uk.bestself.co/collections/featured/products/self-journal) and Cal Newport's "Deep Work".

This is a simple tool to help me plan my day every morning. It's a bit quicker than writing it down and avoids me writing the same goals out every day.

It's built with some simple html & javascript and uses local storage, so no backend. Very much a quick prototype knocked up to scratch an itch.

If I was going to revisit it, I'd restyle it to something a bit more sophisticated and lean into the goal tracking side of it. Let you set long term and medium term goals, flag when a task you complete today contributes to that goal.
